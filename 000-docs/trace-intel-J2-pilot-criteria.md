## Existing requirements versus new proposals

The live canonical program already requires:

- **J3 (`h08j.8.128`):** at least one day of fixture traces through D1–D5, no failed ingests in that run, a trace count meeting J2's threshold, and F2 reflecting the coverage.
- **I14 (`h08j.8.124`):** at least seven consecutive days on a real, non-fixture agent/repository; at least 100 traces ingested, 10 human-accepted annotations, one signed evidence receipt, and successful Mission Control rendering before J14 adoption.
- **Program invariants:** no policy violations, no unreviewed AI proposals promoted into canonical state, retained original traces and append-only annotation history. J14 owner adoption remains separate.

**New proposals requiring owner approval:** at least 20 distinct fixture traces during the separate fixture day; real-ingestion success at least 99%; no unresolved trace gaps; daily projection reconciliation; daily evidence freshness; at least one derived regression and one evaluation during the real window. These numbers are not preexisting standards and cannot silently replace later ratified contracts. A stricter applicable contract wins; incompatible requirements must be resolved before kickoff.

## Counting and time rules

A window is seven continuous 24-hour intervals beginning at the approved UTC start time, not seven selected successful dates. Require at least one eligible real run in every interval and at least 100 distinct accepted real traces across the window. A trace is counted once by its canonical identity and source lineage; retries, duplicates and replayed receipts do not inflate the count. Fixtures, stubs, deliberate fault injections and imported historical traces have separate ledgers and contribute zero real operational credit.

The acquisition ledger records every eligible real source trace offered for ingestion, including rejected and timed-out attempts, with source/run identity, timestamps, disposition and immutable original receipt reference. Let **D** be distinct eligible source traces offered in the approved window and **N** the subset durably accepted, normalized and reconcilable to originals. Proposed ingestion success is `N / D >= 0.99`, with `D > 0`, `N >= 100`, and no unresolved missing trace at close. Refused attempts stay in the attempt ledger, including malformed offers; a refusal with a resolvable eligible source identity remains in D. A duplicate attempt points to that same identity and earns no additional N or D. An offer whose identity cannot be reconciled is an unresolved measurement gap, not a reason to shrink D. Also report total transport attempts, retry counts and first-attempt failures separately; recovery cannot erase a failed attempt. An evaluation's legitimate fail verdict is not an ingestion failure.

Each counted annotation must have a distinct accepted record, authorized human reviewer identity, decision timestamp and source-trace lineage in the append-only audit log. Ten labels copied from one accepted record do not count as ten approvals. AI proposals remain proposals until a human accepts them; zero unauthorized promotions is a hard invariant, not an average rate.

A missing source ledger, missing human-acceptance proof, unaccounted ingest, missing daily real activity or unrecoverable gap means that day cannot earn continuity credit. Proposed reversible-outage exception: a projection-only outage may retain continuity only if restored within one hour, the durable source ledger remains complete throughout, daily reconciliation succeeds and no security or integrity invariant was violated. Record its actual duration and recovery receipt. A longer outage or failed proof invalidates the window; never interpolate missing observations. Preserve the failed window and all receipts. After repair and accountable operator review, begin a new full seven-day window; never splice healthy intervals or silently backfill synthetic credit. Correctly recorded rejected acquisition attempts may be recovered within the window and remain in the denominator, but unknown telemetry cannot be assumed healthy.

## Separate fixture qualification

After prerequisite approval, use one continuous fixture day with at least 20 distinct fixture traces and zero failed ingests for the normal fixture cohort. Record a separate adversarial cohort for duplicate replay, missing lineage, rejected unauthorized annotation and invalid evidence; expected rejections are required controls and are not relabeled as successful normal ingests. F2 must reflect the qualified fixture scope within its existing one-hour requirement. Fixture qualification cannot start or shorten the real seven-day clock.

## Abort, failure and restart

Immediately stop new pilot acquisitions/proposals on any unreviewed AI promotion, unauthorized trace access, credential exposure, unrecovered data corruption, acceptance of invalid evidence, cross-agent/repository contamination, unapproved policy change or inability to preserve originals. Freeze promotion/consumption of suspect outputs, retain receipts and follow the [incident runbook](./trace-intel-incident-runbook.md). Do not continue merely to finish the observation window.

A missing projection/ledger, missing annotation approval proof, failed daily reconciliation or absent real activity pauses new pilot work. Continuity fails except for the explicitly bounded, fully evidenced projection-only recovery above. A valid evaluation failure is retained and blocks its existing gate; it is not evidence that instrumentation failed. Missing measurement is unknown, never a pass. A later repair does not erase the failure record. Restart requires the accountable operator's documented remediation review and a new full window, plus owner approval when scope, policy, budget or trust changes.

## Bounded rollback and acceptance decision

Stop only the newly enabled pilot acquisition, annotation proposal and projection integration paths using the pre-enrolled disable procedure. Keep existing CI, evaluator, repository protections and release gates operational. Do not switch existing consumers to unverified old data, suppress failures, delete evidence, rewrite annotation history or revert unrelated source changes. If evidence integrity is uncertain, existing delivery remains held by its normal gates until an authorized recovery establishes trust.

Retain original and normalized traces, failed-window records, signed receipts and append-only annotation history. Record the disabled integration version, last trusted receipt, operator action and restoration verification; do not invent a deployment command before the architecture and host choices are approved. A rehearsal of that exact bounded procedure is a prerequisite to a real pilot start.

The closing decision must enumerate every criterion as pass, fail or unmeasured, cite the frozen receipts, and state any unresolved gap. Passing this plan provides input to the program reviews; it is not J14 adoption and does not open J15 expansion.
